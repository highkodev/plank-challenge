January challenge 2027: 30 days, 1 to 30 January

Start the new year together: day 1 on 1 January, day 30 on 30 January, and on 31 January the max test shows how much longer you can hold.

  • By 31 December: install the free app from the Connect IQ Store and do the max test (forearm plank as long as you can). The watch suggests your level.
  • 1 to 30 January: one workout a day, about 6 to 26 minutes depending on level and day. Rest days fall on 6, 13, 19 and 25 January; check them off with START.
  • Missed a day? Just carry on the next day. The plan counts the days you have done, not calendar days.
  • 31 January: the max test again, rested. The watch shows how much longer you hold than at the start. A short review in the Connect IQ Store helps others find the challenge.

How a workout works

The colour of the ring on the watch shows which phase you are in.

Countdown 10 sWarm-up 2:30RestPlank setRest…Rest 10 sCobra 30 s

Each rest is as long as the day's set length, at most 30 s, so a little longer after shorter, harder sets. Before the stretch, 10 s are enough to lie down. During the rest the watch shows the next exercise with a short cue; if you need longer, DOWN extends the rest once by 10 s. The watch vibrates halfway through each set and warm-up exercise and beeps during the last 3 seconds. Warm-up and stretch can be turned off under Settings.

Health

Brace abs and glutes, keep breathing, don't strain. Hips sagging? Go to your knees. Pain means stop. Back, heart or blood pressure problems, or pregnant? Ask a doctor first.

A burning feeling in the muscles is normal, pain in the back, shoulders or wrists is not. Better a shorter, clean set than a long one with sagging hips. With wrist problems, prefer the forearm variations.

Technique: In every exercise, brace your abs as if you were about to take a light punch, without sucking them in, and squeeze your glutes. Only so hard that you can still breathe calmly. If your hips sag, finish the set on your knees (side plank: on the lower knee) instead of holding on with a sagging back. In controlled exercises, hold the end position instead of swinging: bird dog 2 to 3 s, the others 1 to 2 s.

Plank exercises

Every workout starts with front, side plank left and right and back; more sets add dynamic variations, ending with a cardio one. In Medium and Pro, harder variations gradually replace the basic exercise in the first 4 sets, while you are still fresh. Demanding exercises are shorter than the day's set length: clean rather than long.

Forearm plank

static

Elbows under shoulders, keep breathing

Forearms on the floor, elbows under the shoulders. Brace abs and glutes, body straight. Keep breathing. If the hips sag, finish on your knees.

Focus: rectus abdominis, deep core, shoulders

Beginner: from day 1, Medium: from day 1, Pro: from day 1

High plank

static

Hands under shoulders, keep breathing

Push-up position, hands under the shoulders. Brace your core, don't let the hips sag. Keep breathing. If the hips sag, finish on your knees.

Focus: core, shoulders, triceps

Beginner: from day 2, Medium: from day 2, Pro: from day 2

Side plank left

static

Left forearm, keep hips up

Left elbow under the shoulder, feet stacked. Lift the hips until the body is straight, top hand on the hip. Brace your abs. If the hips sag, finish on the lower knee.

Focus: obliques, left hip

Beginner: from day 1, Medium: from day 1, Pro: from day 1

Side plank right

static

Right forearm, keep hips up

Right elbow under the shoulder, feet stacked. Lift the hips until the body is straight, top hand on the hip. Brace your abs. If the hips sag, finish on the lower knee.

Focus: obliques, right hip

Beginner: from day 1, Medium: from day 1, Pro: from day 1

Shoulder taps

dynamic

High plank, tap opposite shoulder

In a high plank, tap the opposite shoulder with one hand, then switch. Brace your abs, keep the hips still.

Focus: anti-rotation, shoulder stability

Beginner: from day 18, Medium: from day 1, Pro: from day 1

Mountain climbers

dynamic

Drive knees to chest, alternating

In a high plank, drive the knees towards the chest, alternating quickly. Shoulders over hands, abs braced, hips low.

Focus: core, hip flexors, cardio

Duration: 75 % of the set length

Beginner: not in plan, Medium: from day 10, Pro: from day 8

Plank jacks

dynamic

Jump feet apart and back together

In a high plank, jump the feet apart and back together. Keep the core stable. Barefoot or with sensitive joints, step the feet out and back in one at a time instead.

Focus: core, hips, cardio

Duration: 75 % of the set length

Beginner: from day 30, Medium: from day 11, Pro: from day 7

Up-down plank

dynamic

Forearms, high plank and back

Push from forearm plank up into a high plank and back down. Abs braced, hips as still as possible. Switch the leading arm regularly.

Focus: shoulders, triceps, core

Duration: 75 % of the set length

Beginner: from day 17, Medium: from day 3, Pro: from day 3

Spiderman plank

dynamic

Bring knee out towards the elbow

In a high plank, bring one knee outside towards the elbow, back, then switch sides. Abs braced, hips low.

Focus: obliques, hip mobility

Duration: 75 % of the set length

Beginner: from day 16, Medium: from day 2, Pro: from day 2

Plank arm reach

dynamic

Forearm plank, reach arms forward

In a forearm plank, reach one arm forward and hold for 1 to 2 s, alternating. Abs braced, hips still, feet slightly wider.

Focus: anti-rotation, shoulder stability

Beginner: from day 12, Medium: from day 3, Pro: from day 3

Plank leg lift

dynamic

Lift straight legs, alternating

In a forearm plank, lift one straight leg, hold for 1 to 2 s, switch. Abs braced, don't tilt the hips.

Focus: glutes, lower back, core

Beginner: from day 10, Medium: from day 1, Pro: from day 1

Reverse plank

static

Face up, hips high, arms straight

Sit with hands behind you, fingers towards the feet. Lift the hips until the body forms a line. Glutes and abs braced.

Focus: posterior chain: glutes, back extensors, shoulders

Duration: 75 % of the set length

Beginner: from day 2, Medium: from day 2, Pro: from day 2

Bird dog

dynamic

All fours, extend opposite arm and leg

On all fours, back straight. Extend one arm and the opposite leg, hold for 2 to 3 s, return, switch sides. Abs braced, slowly, without arching the back.

Focus: back extensors, glutes, core stability (easy on the back)

Beginner: from day 1, Medium: from day 1, Pro: from day 1

Long-lever plank

staticadvanced

Elbows ahead of shoulders, squeeze glutes

Like the forearm plank, but with the elbows a hand's width ahead of the shoulders. Brace glutes and abs hard, don't let the hips sag.

Focus: rectus abdominis, deep core (longer lever)

Replaces Forearm plank in the first 4 sets.

Duration: 50 % of the set length

Beginner: not in plan, Medium: from day 8, Pro: from day 3

Bird dog plank

dynamicadvanced

High plank, lift opposite arm and leg

High plank with the feet a little wider. Lift one arm and the opposite leg, hold for 1 to 2 s, return, switch sides. Abs braced, hips still.

Focus: anti-rotation, core, shoulders, glutes

Replaces High plank in the first 4 sets.

Duration: 75 % of the set length

Beginner: not in plan, Medium: from day 14, Pro: from day 7

Side leg raise left

dynamicadvanced

Left forearm, raise and lower top leg

Side plank on the left forearm. Slowly raise the straight top leg and lower it again, hips stay up, abs braced.

Focus: obliques, side glutes

Replaces Side plank left in the first 4 sets.

Duration: 50 % of the set length

Beginner: not in plan, Medium: from day 20, Pro: from day 11

Side leg raise right

dynamicadvanced

Right forearm, raise and lower top leg

Side plank on the right forearm. Slowly raise the straight top leg and lower it again, hips stay up, abs braced.

Focus: obliques, side glutes

Replaces Side plank right in the first 4 sets.

Duration: 50 % of the set length

Beginner: not in plan, Medium: from day 20, Pro: from day 11

Reverse plank leg lift

dynamicadvanced

Hips high, lift legs alternately

Reverse plank on straight arms. Lift one straight leg to hip height, hold for 1 to 2 s, switch. Hips stay high and level. With shoulder pain, hold the normal reverse plank instead.

Focus: glutes, hamstrings, back extensors, anti-rotation

Replaces Reverse plank in the first 4 sets.

Duration: 50 % of the set length

Beginner: not in plan, Medium: from day 26, Pro: from day 14

To finish

Cobra stretch

stretch

Face down, gently push chest up

Face down, hands next to the chest. Gently push the upper body up, hips stay down. Breathe calmly.

Focus: abs, hip flexors (counter-movement)

Do the test after day 30 rested, not right after the last workout.

Privacy

The app runs only on the watch and never connects to the internet. It stores progress, statistics, settings, test results and how you felt on the watch; if you delete the app, they are gone.

If Save is on under Settings, the watch saves every workout as an activity, which Garmin Connect transfers like any other: with heart rate and the app's fields (Feel, Effort, Exercises, Sets, Longest set, Plank time). Garmin's privacy policy applies to that.

The developer receives no data: no accounts, no ads, no analytics. This help page is hosted on GitHub Pages without cookies or third-party content; GitHub processes technical data such as the IP address when the page is loaded.

Structure and progression follow established literature on core and strength training. The app is no substitute for advice from a doctor or physiotherapist.

  • McGill S. Low Back Disorders: Evidence-Based Prevention and Rehabilitation. 3rd ed. Human Kinetics, 2016.
    core endurance; side plank and bird dog as back-friendly basics; short, clean holds
  • McGill S. Ultimate Back Fitness and Performance. 6th ed. Backfitpro, 2017.
    progression through harder variations instead of longer holds
  • Grenier SG, McGill SM. Quantification of lumbar stability by using 2 different abdominal activation strategies. Arch Phys Med Rehabil. 2007;88:54-62.
    Abdominal bracing gave more lumbar stability than hollowing in the model (small lab study)
  • Golob I, Opara Zupančič M, Kozinc Ž. Abdominal Hollowing vs. Abdominal Bracing: A Scoping Review of Clinical Trials on Effectiveness for Trunk Stability and Rehabilitation. J Funct Morphol Kinesiol. 2024;9(4):193. doi:10.3390/jfmk9040193
    Hollowing and bracing both improve trunk stability, neither is clearly superior
  • Schoenfeld BJ, Contreras B, Tiryaki-Sonmez G, Willardson JM, Fontana F. An electromyographic comparison of a modified version of the plank with a long lever and posterior tilt versus the traditional plank exercise. Sports Biomechanics. 2014;13(3):296-306. doi:10.1080/14763141.2014.942355
    long-lever plank: clearly more abdominal activity than the regular forearm plank
  • Boren K, Conrey C, Le Coguic J, Paprocki L, Voight M, Robinson TK. Electromyographic analysis of gluteus medius and gluteus maximus during rehabilitation exercises. Int J Sports Phys Ther. 2011;6(3):206-223. PMID 22034614
    side plank with leg raise: high activity of the gluteus medius
  • Ekstrom RA, Donatelli RA, Carp KC. Electromyographic analysis of core trunk, hip, and thigh muscles during 9 rehabilitation exercises. J Orthop Sports Phys Ther. 2007;37(12):754-762.
    single-leg bridge: about twice the gluteal activity of the two-leg bridge. Basis for the reverse plank leg lift
  • Zahiri A, Alizadeh S, Daneshjoo A, Pike N, Konrad A, Behm DG. Core muscle activation with foam rolling and static planks. Front Physiol. 2022;13:852094. doi:10.3389/fphys.2022.852094
    reverse plank: strongest activation of the back extensors among the planks tested
  • Haff GG, Triplett NT (eds.). Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning. 4th ed. NSCA / Human Kinetics, 2016.
    exercise order: demanding exercises first, alternating load, rest periods
  • Bosquet L, Montpetit J, Arvisais D, Mujika I. Effects of tapering on performance: a meta-analysis. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 2007;39(8):1358-1365. doi:10.1249/mss.0b013e31806010e0
    less volume before a performance test improves the result, so do the max test after day 30 rested
